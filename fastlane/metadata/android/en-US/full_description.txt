<p>OpenOBD is a minimal app for ELM327-compatible Bluetooth OBD-II adapters, such as the Veepeak OBDCheck BLE and BLE+. It connects over Bluetooth LE without pairing, or over Classic Bluetooth.</p>
<ul>
<li><b>Live:</b> gauges you choose and every sensor the car reports. Record to CSV.</li>
<li><b>Codes:</b> stored, pending and permanent trouble codes with descriptions, freeze frame, emissions readiness, and a guarded clear.</li>
<li><b>Vehicle:</b> VIN, control units, calibration IDs, and the car's own monitor test results with pass/fail limits, and a check of which ELM327 commands your adapter really supports.</li>
<li><b>Care:</b> a service schedule by distance and time.</li>
<li><b>Terminal:</b> send any AT or OBD command to the adapter.</li>
<li><b>Demo:</b> try it without an adapter.</li>
</ul>
<p>No network permission, no account, no analytics, no ads. Standard OBD-II only: ABS, airbag, coding and service resets are outside what a generic adapter offers.</p>
